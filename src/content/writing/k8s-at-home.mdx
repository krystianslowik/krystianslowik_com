---
title: k8s at home, and why I run it
blurb: I use a three-node homelab to meet failures before they reach a customer.
pubDate: 2025-12-18
---

Three Ryzen mini-PCs sit in a cupboard in Münster. They run kubeadm on Ubuntu:
one control plane, two workers, Longhorn with three replicas, Calico and a
Cloudflare Tunnel. The router exposes no public load balancer.

I can break this cluster at 11pm without keeping a customer waiting. That makes
it a useful place to test upgrades and failure modes before I meet them at work.

The cluster has taught me a few specific lessons:

- The last rolling OS upgrade crossed all three nodes with no data loss.
- Draining the control-plane node used to wake a small wake-on-LAN helper. I now
  scale the helper to zero before maintenance.
- AMD idle states caused a healthy node to panic at 3am. All three nodes now use
  the C-state fix, and the panic has not returned.

I learned each of those from the logs, then added the fix to the runbook.
