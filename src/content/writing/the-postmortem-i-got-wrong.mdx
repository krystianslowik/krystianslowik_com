---
title: The postmortem I got wrong
blurb: I blamed a configuration value. The review process was the real problem.
pubDate: 2026-02-03
---

My first postmortem blamed a bad configuration value shipped late on Friday.
The explanation was tidy and wrong.

The value was a symptom. A load-bearing change passed review without a question.
We fixed the value, then the same review gap caused another incident a month
later.

I rewrote the postmortem and changed how I review incident reports. I now look
for the process that allowed the immediate cause through.
