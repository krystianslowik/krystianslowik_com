---
title: Boring is a feature
blurb: Why I optimise systems for the absence of surprise, and what that costs in perceived ambition.
pubDate: 2026-05-12
---

The best compliment a system can get is silence. No pages, no war rooms, no
heroics — just the thing doing its job while everyone forgets it exists.

That's a hard thing to put on a performance review. "Made the on-call rotation
uneventful" doesn't sparkle next to "shipped a new platform." But the boring
work is what lets the exciting work survive contact with production.

This is a note about optimising for the absence of surprise — and the quiet
career cost of being the person who keeps the lights on.
