---
title: Boring is a feature
blurb: I optimise for quiet systems. That work is hard to show on a performance review.
pubDate: 2026-05-12
---

The best compliment a system can get is silence. It does its job and nobody gets
paged. The team stops thinking about it.

Performance reviews reward visible launches. Quiet reliability work often
disappears from the record, even when it keeps the platform running.
